ABOUT US

A quiet place in
the mountains.

Icchē Neer is a small, welcoming homestay in Icchēgaon for travellers who want to slow down and enjoy the hills.

Stay in a comfortable room, enjoy the fresh mountain air, watch the changing colours of the Kanchenjunga range and explore the surrounding villages at your own pace.

Our focus is simple: clean rooms, thoughtful hospitality, good local food and help whenever you need it.
